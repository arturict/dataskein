# DataSkein v0.1.6

This release gives the public landing page a complete product-focused redesign.

## What changed

- Leads with the concrete job: turn awkward files into reproducible answers.
- Shows the real DataSkein workspace directly in the hero.
- Adds product-level views of profiling, visible recipes, charts, and portable exports.
- Explains the complete file-to-answer workflow and local-only architecture.
- Documents the current 1 GB per-file limit and answers common privacy and reproducibility questions.
- Refreshes the repository screenshot and Open Graph preview.
- Moves screenshot and E2E preview servers to strict, dedicated ports.

No workspace behavior or file-handling limits changed in this release.
